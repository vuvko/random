## Forests

Even though the islands seem not to be a big piece of land, the forests there differ across the different parts.
When you travel from the settlement, the view changes dramatically depending on where you move: north, east, or south (the settlement is on the west coast), or onto another island.

I speculate that this is based on the presence of lakes, swamps, and the history of aggressive forest cuts during the prison camp period.
Some of the roads are in almost-perfect condition (and one of them follows an old railroad).
But as you go further from the settlement, the condition deteriorates. Mostly near swamps, where the water, trying to flow from one part to another, erodes everything.

## Lakes

There are no rivers on the islands, but a lot of lakes at different elevation levels.
Those lakes were connected via channels to create a robust fresh-water supply for the settlement.
And there is a boat-renting service on the island for traveling that lake system.

I highly recommend renting a boat for yourself and going to different parts of that system, as there are interesting views.
Beware of the gulls during their nesting season, as there are a lot of nests on some lakes, and the birds don't like intruders in their homes.
