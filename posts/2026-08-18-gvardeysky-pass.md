# Where to go in Almaty. Gvardeysky Pass

When I went to [Kok Zhailau](./2026-08-16-kok-zhailau.md) via a different route, I saw an interesting hiking route I had not known about before.
A loop through Gvardeysky Pass to the [Old Japanese Road](./2026-08-05-japanese-road.md), with a small detour for a great view from Observation Mountain.

The start was the same as the previous route to Kok Zhailau, near the Kazachka river at [43.129373, 76.952791](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?lat=43.129373&lon=76.952791&zoom=17#map=17/43.129369/76.952791), around 1700 m.
The highest point was around 2400 m near Observation Mountain (which itself is 2350 m).
You can choose your descent: you come down onto part of the Old Japanese Road, and from there you can either descend to the Ayusai visitor centre (gentler, but a rather long road) or to the bus station near the checkpoint (much steeper, but both closer to the city and faster).

After this photo the rain started, and I packed my Canon camera away.
I took the other two pictures with my Samsung A53 phone.
There were not that many new angles, and I was getting tired of climbing down.
So, if you want, you can read more about the Old Japanese Road in my other [post](./2026-08-05-japanese-road.md).
The descent has some amazing views, but was rather scary.
Especially when you need to go around a rock and you can clearly see there is nothing that will slow your fall if you misstep.

![The view you get from the descent is breathtaking. Both because it looks cool and because it is scary to look down from here.](./images/gvardeysky-pass/photo_2026-08-18_18-57-35.webp)
![Some ruins of unknown purpose.](./images/gvardeysky-pass/photo_2026-08-18_18-57-55.webp)

We took the longer way, along the whole Old Japanese Road.
And we spent a lot of the time looking up, not believing that we had come down from the top of the range.
