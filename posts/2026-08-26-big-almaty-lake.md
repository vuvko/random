# Where to go in Almaty. Big Almaty Lake

Another post about one-day hiking near Almaty.
This route is quite popular, but these days it is tricky to get to if you don't have a car.

[Big Almaty Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Almaty_Lake) is located inside the [Ile-Alatau National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ile-Alatau_National_Park), and there is an additional checkpoint (around [43.08583, 76.97006](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?lat=43.08583&lon=76.97006&zoom=16#map=16/43.08582/76.97006)) beyond which only pedestrians are allowed.

The famous Krugosvetka ("round-the-world") trail starts from the eastern gorge near Medeo and finishes here.](./images/big-almaty-lake/IMG_1086.webp)

## The Ascent

We got to the checkpoint by taxi, quickly dismissed the suggestion to pay for a ride to the top, and started our journey.
We first thought we would ascend all the way up using only the asphalt road, but on a whim we decided to try climbing near the water pipe (where most people ascend and descend the trail).

The weather was perfect at the top.
We got some rain climbing up, but almost as soon as we saw the lake the clouds started to disappear, and we rested under the Sun with a great view of the water.

## The Descent

After some time, we started the descent.
We decided we didn't want to climb down the same way we came up, so we descended by road as much as possible.

After that we kept descending until we reached the Old Japanese Road, where we rested near the Big Almaty river.
We continued to the Ayusai visitor centre and beyond, down to the bus station, where we finally caught a taxi willing to take us home.

If you plan to visit Big Almaty Lake, I suggest the same route we chose: ascend near the water pipe, and descend by road.
That way you will experience different views, and see not only Big Almaty Lake but also the Kumbel river and the gorge from several angles.
