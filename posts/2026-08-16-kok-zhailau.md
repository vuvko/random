# Where to go in Almaty. Kok Zhailau

*Published: 2026-08-16*

[Continuing](./2026-08-05-japanese-road.md) on where to hike near Almaty, especially if you are a beginner and enjoy a more relaxed experience.

One of the most popular hiking places is the Kok Zhailau plateau.
There are several ways to climb there from the city; the most popular is the eastern path that starts at the Ak Bulak bus station ([43.162129, 77.040891](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?lat=43.162129&lon=77.040891&zoom=18#map=18/43.162131/77.040891)).
But I wanted to try another start, near the Kazachka river ([43.129373, 76.952791](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?lat=43.129373&lon=76.952791&zoom=17#map=17/43.129369/76.952791)).
The only way to reach that western point directly is by car; otherwise you can start from the checkpoint near another bus station ([43.128900, 76.906309](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?lat=43.128900&lon=76.906309&zoom=18#map=18/43.128898/76.906309)) and walk up from there.
Another problem is that there is no mobile signal near that point.
So if you decide to descend that way, you will most likely have to walk almost all the way down to the checkpoint.

So this post is about the hike that can be described as Kazachka — Kok Zhailau — Ak Bulak, as I descended to a different place from the one I started at.
